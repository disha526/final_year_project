# CCTV Crime & Anomaly Detection Surveillance Benchmark
This dataset integrates authentic multi-angle CCTV surveillance footage from public benchmarks:
1. Simuletic CCTV ATM Robbery & Gun/Knife Threat Benchmark (2024)
2. Simuletic CCTV Aggressive Poses & Physical Fight Benchmark
3. Simuletic CCTV Knife Assault & Armed Threat Benchmark
4. Simuletic CCTV Fire, Smoke & Arson Detection Benchmark
5. DCSASS Retail Store CCTV Shoplifting Benchmark
6. UCF-Crime Real-world Surveillance Anomaly Benchmark
7. Simuletic 1K Pedestrian Surveillance Baseline (Normal Activity)

All bounding boxes are standardized to YOLO format [class_id x_center y_center width height].
Compatible with YOLOv8, YOLOv9, YOLOv10, and YOLO11.
